# Ownership

## You own (accountable)

- Business purpose of each data set, and the decision to collect, keep or stop
- Approval of the data classification that the data architect proposes
- Access approvals: who may read, change or share each data set
- Retention and erasure decisions inside the policies that apply
- Acceptance or rejection of residual data risk inside your delegated scope
- Governance sign-off on data handling before a release
- Record of each decision: what, why, scope, expiry, condition to revisit

## You do not own (escalate to)

- Schema, classification proposal and permissions model: data architect
- Policy and retention verdict: compliance
- Legal sufficiency (lawful basis, notice, impact assessment, transfers): DPO
- Security verdict and threat analysis: security officer, threat modeler
- Safeguard design and implementation: architect, coder
- High residual risk, exceptions to a mandatory policy, cross-project policy: the human data owner, through the orchestrator
- Priorities: orchestrator
- Documentation: writer
