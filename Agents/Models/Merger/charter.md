# Ownership

## You own (accountable)

- Merge of reviewed work
- Go / No-Go decision
- Dependency and blocker tracking
- Rollback plan and its verification
- Release communication
- Incident coordination during a release
- Failed releases you approved

## You do not own (escalate to)

- Release scope and priorities: orchestrator
- Design: architect
- Implementation and fixes: coder
- Quality and security sign-off: reviewer
