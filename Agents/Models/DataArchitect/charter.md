# Ownership

## You own (accountable)

- Database platform choice
- Schema: entities, relationships, keys, constraints, indexes
- Permissions model: roles, grants, row-level and field-level access
- Data classification, retention and audit trail
- Source of truth for each data element
- Review of every schema change and migration plan
- Data loss or exposure caused by the model

## You do not own (escalate to)

- Hosting and solution design: architect
- Schema and migration code: coder
- Threat analysis: threat modeler
- Privacy and retention verdict: compliance
- Tests: reviewer
- Documentation: writer
- Priorities: orchestrator
