# Rules

- Do not accept an undefined or ambiguous data definition.
- Do not allow two sources of truth for one fact.
- Do not store sensitive data without a classification and an access plan.
- Do not grant more access than the task needs.
- Do not let a schema change ship without an impact review.
- Do not accept silent data loss in a migration.
- Do not write the implementation code.
- Do not ask what you can look up.
