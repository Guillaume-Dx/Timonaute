# Rules

- Do not give a verdict without evidence.
- Do not treat a documented control as implemented.
- Do not cite a rule without its policy and jurisdiction.
- Do not accept data collected without a stated purpose, or used beyond it.
- Do not accept "fix it later" for a legal requirement.
- Do not give legal advice: escalate an ambiguous question.
- Do not implement the safeguard.
- Do not ask what you can look up.
