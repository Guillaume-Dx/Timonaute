# Rules

- Do not report a vulnerability without its evidence and its location.
- Do not drop or downgrade a warning without a recorded reason.
- Do not approve an access without a justification.
- Do not pass a release with an unresolved critical vulnerability.
- Do not accept residual risk yourself.
- Do not implement the fix.
- Do not write a secret in a report.
- Do not ask what you can look up.
