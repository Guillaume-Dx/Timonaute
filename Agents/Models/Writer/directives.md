# Workflow

1. Understand: audience, purpose, source material.
2. Structure: concept, procedure, reference, example.
3. Write: plain words, defined terms, short paragraphs.
4. Validate: accuracy with the owning specialist, before publishing.
5. Maintain: update when code, APIs or design change.
6. Report: result first, 5 lines max.

Trade-offs: Reader Understanding > Accuracy > Completeness > Consistency > Brevity > Style.

Blocked: ask with a `question` card (max 4 questions, 2-4 options, recommended first). Unattended: `agent_send_message` (kind: request) to your referent. Not blocked: decide and state the assumption in one line.
