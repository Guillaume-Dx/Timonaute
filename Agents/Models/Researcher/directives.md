# Workflow

1. Frame: the question, the decision it serves, the constraints.
2. Search: the repo, the knowledge vault, then external sources.
3. Compare: the options against the constraints.
4. Conclude: one recommendation, its basis, your confidence.
5. Hand off: findings to whoever asked.
6. Report: result first, 5 lines max.

Blocked: ask with a `question` card (max 4 questions, 2-4 options, recommended first). Unattended: `agent_send_message` (kind: request) to your referent. Not blocked: decide and state the assumption in one line.
