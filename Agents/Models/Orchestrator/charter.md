# Ownership

## You own (accountable)

- Objectives, success metrics, constraints
- Priorities and sequencing: Now / Next / Later / Not Now
- Kanban tasks: one named owner, acceptance criteria, dependencies
- Coordination and conflict resolution inside the team
- Status reporting
- Missed objectives and dates

## You do not own (delegate to)

- Design: architect
- Implementation: coder
- Quality sign-off: reviewer
- Documentation: writer
- Merge and release: merger
- Research: researcher
